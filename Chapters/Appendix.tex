This glossary contains the most important mathematical, physical and musical vocabulary used in this course.

\section*{Basic Acoustics}

\begin{tabular}{p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.38\textwidth}}
\textbf{English} & \textbf{Deutsch} & \textbf{Explanation} \\ \hline
acoustics & Akustik & science of sound \\
sound & Schall / Klang & mechanical vibrations travelling through a medium \\
sound wave & Schallwelle & wave transporting sound \\
vibration & Vibration & repeated back-and-forth motion \\
oscillation & Schwingung & periodic motion around an equilibrium position \\
medium & Medium & material through which a wave e.g. sound travels (air, water, solids) \\
elastic & elastisch & returns to its original shape after deformation \\
frequency & Frequenz & number of oscillations per second (Hz) \\
pitch & Tonhöhe & how high or low a sound is perceived \\
tone & Ton & sound with one dominant frequency \\
timbre & Klangfarbe & characteristic sound of an instrument/tone \\
auditory system & Hörsystem & ear and brain together \\
perception & Wahrnehmung & how the brain interprets sound \\
\end{tabular}

\section*{Oscillations}

\begin{tabular}{p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.38\textwidth}}
\textbf{English} & \textbf{Deutsch} & \textbf{Explanation} \\ \hline
harmonic oscillator & harmonischer Oszillator & ideal system performing periodic motion \\
equilibrium position & Gleichgewichtslage & resting position \\
restoring force & Rückstellkraft & force pulling an object back to equilibrium \\
displacement & Auslenkung & distance from equilibrium \\

\section*{Waves}

\begin{tabular}{p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.38\textwidth}}
\textbf{English} & \textbf{Deutsch} & \textbf{Explanation} \\ \hline
wave & Welle & disturbance travelling through space \\

\section*{Standing Waves}

\begin{tabular}{p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.38\textwidth}}
\textbf{English} & \textbf{Deutsch} & \textbf{Explanation} \\ \hline
superposition & Superposition & adding several waves together \\
interference & Interferenz & overlap of waves causing a new characteric shape \\
constructive interference & konstruktive Interferenz & amplitudes become larger \\
destructive interference & destruktive Interferenz & amplitudes cancel each other \\
standing wave & stehende Welle & wave pattern that stays in place, all oscillators move in phase\\
travelling wave & laufende Welle & wave moving through space \\
node & Wellenknoten & point that never moves \\
antinode & Wellenbauch & point with maximum amplitude \\

fixed end & festes Ende & The end cannot move, so it is always a node.\\
free end & freies Ende & An end that can move freely, so an antinode always forms there. \\
