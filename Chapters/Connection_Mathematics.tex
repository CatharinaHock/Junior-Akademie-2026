\section{Connection to Mathematics}

We can now clearly see how mathematics, physics, and music are connected:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Sine and cosine functions describe waves
    \item Superposition explains complex sounds
    \item Exponential functions describe musical tuning
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Final Insight:}
Mathematics is not only abstract — it helps us understand and describe the sounds we hear every day.
